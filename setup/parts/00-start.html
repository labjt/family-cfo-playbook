You are not learning a system. You are installing one.

The written playbook has 38 procedures. You are not going to memorise them. You are going to paste most of them into Hazel, and Hazel will run them for you.

How this works

  • Every step below is a few minutes of clicking and pasting. Nothing needs to be written by you.
  • Tick a step when it is done. Your progress is saved in this browser.
  • Do them in order. Each phase ends with something that actually works.
  • You can stop after any phase and come back. Phase 1 alone is worth doing.

What you will have at the end

  • Hazel prepares an agenda before every client meeting, sitting on your calendar.
  • Hazel joins the meeting, takes the notes in your format, and drafts the follow-up email.
  • The seven-area client profile fills itself in from meetings, email and your CRM.
  • Your planning method, written down once, applied to every plan Hazel drafts.
  • You review and approve. Hazel never sends anything to a client on its own.
The honest limit

Hazel has no idea that a Discovery meeting comes before an Investment Plan meeting. It has no concept of a sequence and it will not stop you skipping a step. The order lives in your head and in the Playbook — Hazel carries the execution, not the discipline.

Before you paste anything

Hazel's screens change often and their own documentation currently contradicts itself in a few places. If a button is not where a step says it is, look for the nearest thing with a similar name — the sequence is right even when a label has moved.
