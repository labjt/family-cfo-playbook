# T02 — Meeting agendas

## Purpose

Ready-to-adapt agendas for every standard meeting. An agenda goes out with the pre-meeting letter (T01), is walked at the opening, and is invited to change.

## How to use

- Copy the block, fill the blanks, cut items that don't apply, keep the standing items (marked ★) from SOP 05: state purpose and ask what would make the meeting successful; ask what has changed; schedule the next meeting in the room.
- Timings are for a ninety-minute meeting; scale.
- Materials column lists what the CSA puts in the packet.

## CCM1 — Discovery (≤ 90 min)

Purpose: to understand what matters most to you and decide together whether we are the right firm.

| Min | Item | Leads | Materials |
|---|---|---|---|
| 5 | Welcome; purpose; ★ what would make this valuable for you; agenda additions | LA | Agenda |
| 3 | How we will work today; consent to record | LA | Consent wording |
| 60 | Profile conversation: values · goals · relationships · assets · advisors · process · interests (+ family overlay / mandate questions) | LA | T03, mind-map sheet |
| 10 | Fit and next steps: the sequence ahead; precommitment | LA | Sequence one-pager |
| 5 | ★ Schedule the investment-plan meeting (+2 weeks); close | LA / CSA | Calendar |

## Rediscovery (≤ 75 min)

Purpose: to make sure we know everything that matters to you now.

| Min | Item | Leads | Materials |
|---|---|---|---|
| 5 | Purpose; ★ success; agenda additions | LA | Prior mind map |
| 45 | Redraw the profile against the prior one; what changed; assets held elsewhere; advisors; rising generation | LA | T03, T04 |
| 15 | What this suggests for the plan; hooks for the expert network | LA | — |
| 5 | Ask for introductions where natural (SOP 50) | LA | — |
| 5 | ★ Schedule the next progress meeting; close | LA / CSA | Calendar |

| Min | Item | Leads | Materials |

| 15 | Questions and concerns; what you'd want changed | LA | — |

| 5 | ★ Schedule the commitment meeting (+1 week); close | LA / CSA | Calendar |

## CCM3 — Mutual Commitment Meeting (≤ 60 min)

Purpose: to commit to each other and put the plan in motion.

| Min | Item | Leads | Materials |
|---|---|---|---|
| 5 | Welcome; ★ success; collect all questions first | LA | Agenda |
| 10 | Answer the questions | LA | Plan |
| 20 | Engagement letter (client, mode, scope, fee, consent); paperwork with sign-here flags; funding instructions | LA / CSA | Engagement letter; T05; account forms |
| 5 | Congratulations; what to expect (buyer's remorse, the media, uneven returns) | LA | — |
| 5 | Introductions: whom else should we be helping? (SOP 50) | LA | — |
| 5 | What happens next: transfers (T07); save every piece of paper for the 45-day meeting | CSA | T07 |
| 5 | ★ Schedule the 45-day follow-up (~6 weeks); close | LA / CSA | Calendar |

## CCM5 — Regular Progress Meeting (first: ≤ 90 min; subsequent: ≤ 75 min)

Purpose: to review progress toward your goals and decide the next steps.

| Min | Item | Leads | Materials |
|---|---|---|---|
| 5 | Welcome; ★ success; walk the agenda; collect questions (answer later) | LA | Agenda |
| 10 | ★ What has happened since we last met — life, family, business | LA | — |
| 15 | Progress against goals; last quarter's figures; the IPS | SA / LA | T11 package |
| 25 | *First meeting:* present the advanced-planning plan (four areas, highest priorities). *Subsequent:* progress against the tracker; the one or two items that matter now | LA (+ specialist if needed) | T10 |
| 10 | Drop / defer / do for each recommendation; next steps | LA | T10 |
| 5 | Coordination update (Mode B) or family update (Mode A) | LA | — |
| 5 | Held-away assets and introductions (SOP 50); annual fee conversation when due | LA | — |
| 5 | ★ Schedule the next meeting at your cadence; close | LA / CSA | Calendar |

## Annual family meeting (half-day or two half-days)

Purpose: to connect, to learn, and to make the decisions the family needs to make together.

| Block | Item | Leads | Materials |
|---|---|---|---|
| Open | Welcome; ground rules; a story from family history or a reading of the mission statement; ★ what would make this a good meeting | Chair / facilitator | T16 |
| Individual track | Round-robin: what is new in each person's life and work; new members introduce themselves | Chair | — |
| Generational track | Each generation caucuses: what we want to share with, and learn from, the other | Facilitator | T16 exercise |
| Family-wide track | Family balance sheet review (five capitals); progress on family-level goals; the advanced-planning tracker at family level; philanthropy report; property and family bank reports | LA / council | T18, T10, T20 |
| Decisions | Items requiring a joint decision; who decides; consensus check | Chair | T14 decision rights |
| Governance | Mission statement re-read; review of representatives; any amendments | Chair | T16 |
| Education | Short learning segment for the rising generation or the whole family | LA / specialist | — |
| Stories | One or two stories, prepared in advance from the prompts | Elder | T16 |
| Close | Takeaway round; gratitude round; next meeting date; thanks | Chair | — |
