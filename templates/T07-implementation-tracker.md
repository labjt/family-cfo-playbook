# T07 — Implementation tracker

## Purpose

One row per account, entity, or trust that is opening, moving, retitling, or changing designations. It is the CSA's daily control sheet during the window between the Mutual Commitment Meeting and the 45-Day Follow-up, and again whenever a progress-meeting decision creates new accounts or structures.

## How to use

- Open it in [CRM] (or the firm's tracking sheet) on the day documents are executed; one tracker per client, one row per account.
- Review every open row each morning; anything past its expected date moves to the exceptions log the same day.
- Close a row only when positions, cash, cost basis, registration, and beneficiary designations reconcile to the plan.
- Print or export the completed tracker into the notebook (T08) under *Implementation*.

## Daily review checklist (CSA)

- [ ] Every open row has a next action, an owner, and a date.
- [ ] Any row past its expected date has been logged as an exception and the client told.
- [ ] Custodian acknowledgements outstanding more than two business days have been chased.
- [ ] Cost-basis requests outstanding more than ten business days have been escalated.
- [ ] Client status update sent on schedule (weekly or per process preference).
- [ ] New documents received today are filed to the [Document vault] and listed in T08.
