# T01 — Client correspondence

## Purpose

The standard letters and notes the practice sends around every meeting, so that a client hears the same voice whoever writes. Each block is fill-in; keep the tone warm, brief, and specific.

## How to use

- CSA drafts from the block; LA signs anything that carries advice or a commitment. Send by the channel the client chose under Process (SOP 11).
- Replace `[Client]`, `[Advisor]`, `[Firm]` and the blanks. Delete lines that don't apply. Never paste a block unedited.
- File every sent item in [CRM] against the meeting.

## 1. Pre-meeting letter (T−7 days)

> Dear [Client],
>
> Thank you for agreeing to meet on ______ at ______ (time) at ______ (place / video link). The purpose of our meeting is ______ (one sentence — e.g., "to understand what matters most to you and your family, and to decide together whether we are the right firm to help").
>
> I've attached a short agenda. Please add anything you'd like us to cover — the meeting is yours.
>
> It would help if you could bring, or send ahead: recent statements for all investment and bank accounts; your last two years' tax returns; trust, partnership, and company documents; insurance schedules; any current investment policy or plan. Don't worry about completeness — bring what is easy and we'll work from there.
>
> Joining me will be ______ (name, role, what they will do in the meeting). We have set aside ______ minutes. Parking / arrival: ______.
>
> I'm looking forward to it.
>
> [Advisor], [Firm]

## 2. Next-meeting confirmation (≤ 24 hours after any meeting)

> Dear [Client],
>
> Thank you for today. As agreed, our next meeting is on ______ at ______ (place / link). Its purpose is ______.
>
> Before then: we will ______ (what the practice will do); you agreed to ______ (what the client will bring or decide). Please call ______ with any questions before then.
>
> [Advisor]

## 3. Post-meeting recap (≤ 24 hours)

> Dear [Client],
>
> A short record of what we decided today so we stay aligned.
>
> **Decisions:** ______ · ______ · ______
>
> **Actions:** ______ (owner, by date) · ______ (owner, by date)
>
> **Documents outstanding:** ______
>
> **Next meeting:** ______ at ______ — purpose: ______.
>
> If I've missed or misstated anything, tell me and I'll correct it.
>
> [Advisor]

## 4. Handwritten notes

| Occasion | Send within | Content |
|---|---|---|
| After an introduction or referral is offered | Same day (next day at the latest) | Thanks; what it means to the practice; you will follow up personally |
| After the commitment meeting | Same day | Congratulations on taking an important step; you are glad to be part of it |
| After a family event (birth, wedding, graduation, loss) | Within a week | Acknowledgement only — no business |
| After a family meeting | Within two days | Thanks for the trust and the candour |

Conventions: fountain pen or good ballpoint; print if handwriting is hard to read; the firm's plain card, no logo larger than a thumbnail; never a marketing enclosure. Gifts follow [Firm Compliance] limits.

## 5. Courtesy notice to a family central team (Mode B, only with the client's consent on T05)

> Dear ______,
>
> [Client] has asked me to let you know that [Firm] has begun working with them on ______ (scope in one line). I understand that your team is responsible for ______, and I'll make sure our work fits within it. Would a short call in the next two weeks suit you to agree how best to coordinate? My direct line is ______.
>
> [Advisor]

## 6. Thank-you after an introduction, and report-back to the referrer

**Thank-you (same day):** see the handwritten note above.

**Report-back (within a week of first contact, then at each milestone):**

> Dear ______,
>
> A quick note to say that I reached ______ as you kindly suggested. We have ______ (met / scheduled a first meeting / agreed to speak next month). I won't share anything about their circumstances, but I wanted you to know your introduction was welcome and is being handled with care. Thank you again.
>
> [Advisor]

Never disclose what the introduced person said, decided, or holds. If they declined, say only that you were grateful for the introduction and left the door open.
