# T12 — Service quality instruments

## Purpose

Five instruments used by SOPs 21, 23 and 25: (a) the practice's own service self-audit in three tiers; (b) the annual client satisfaction survey; (c) the service-failure log; (d) the lost-client interview guide; (e) the Mode B coordination check. All items are original to this practice.

## How to use

- (a) is scored by the LA with the team each May before the survey goes out, and again in November as a mid-year check. Score every item **met / partly / not**; every "partly" or "not" gets an owner and a date.
- (b) goes to every client household in late May per SOP 25; both spouses may answer separately.
- (c) is a running log in [CRM]; every failure is entered within an hour of discovery.
- (d) is used by the LA within two weeks of any client giving notice.
- (e) is sent, with the client's knowledge, to the FCT contact of each Mode B relationship alongside the survey.

- What is one thing the team should start doing, stop doing, or do differently?

## (d) Lost-client interview guide

Thirty minutes, by the LA, within two weeks of notice. Open by thanking them and saying the purpose is to learn, not to persuade.

1. When did you begin to think about making a change — was there a moment, or did it build?
2. What did we miss, or what did we do, that mattered most in the decision?
3. What did you need from us that you were not getting?
4. How did the team's communication feel over the last year — too much, too little, wrong kind?
5. What will the new arrangement give you that we did not?
6. What would you tell a friend about working with us?
7. Is there anything that would make you reconsider, now or later?
8. Is there anything we should do differently for other families?

Close: thank them; confirm how the transition will be handled; leave the door open. Record answers the same day; bring to the next results session (SOP 25).

## (e) Mode B coordination check

Sent to the family central team's designated contact with the client's knowledge; three questions, a 1–5 scale and a comment box.

1. When our team needs something from yours, or you from ours, is it handled promptly and through the right person?
2. Does the information you receive from us about the individual(s) we serve arrive in the form, and within the limits, you expect?
3. In the last year, did anything reach the family from us that surprised you or contradicted your own communication? If so, what?
