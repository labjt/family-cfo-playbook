# T09 — Expert-network meeting pack

## Purpose

Everything the network needs a week before a meeting: the timed agenda, a one-page brief per family, a scenario worksheet per case, the attendance and conflicts declaration, and the output checklist that turns the meeting into a draft advanced-planning map.

## How to use

- AA drafts one brief and one worksheet per family from the Total Client Profile and T10; LA edits; CSA circulates the pack one week ahead with the agenda.
- Use the case code, not the family name, wherever consent on T05 / T14 is limited. Keep the key to codes in [CRM], never in the circulated pack.
- After the meeting, the completed worksheets become the draft map in T10.

## Agenda (90 minutes, up to three cases; scale for more)

| Time | Item | Lead |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00 | Purpose, confidentiality rule, conflicts confirmed | LA |
| 0:05 | Case 1 — brief (10) · issues (10) · scenarios (15) · opportunities and priority (10) · owners and next steps (5) | LA / all |
| 0:55 | Case 2 — same structure, 25 minutes | LA / all |
| 1:20 | Case 3 — same structure, 25 minutes (or park to next meeting) | LA / all |
| 1:45 | What worked, what to change; next meeting date | all |

## Output checklist (CSA, same day)

- [ ] Every issue on each worksheet has a status, an owner, and a dated next step.
- [ ] Draft map per family sent to the assigned experts within three days.
- [ ] Items requiring an expert at the family's progress meeting flagged in [CRM].
- [ ] Anything the family should hear before that meeting noted for the LA.
- [ ] Minutes filed; conflicts declarations filed.
- [ ] Next meeting date confirmed with all core members.
