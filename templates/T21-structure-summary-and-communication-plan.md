# T21 — Structure summary and communication plan

## Purpose

One page, in plain language, for every new trust, entity, gift program, insurance arrangement, or plan the practice helps create. The first half explains what was made and why; the second half plans how the family will hear about it. Planning usually ends in a flurry of documents nobody can later explain — this page is the antidote.

## How to use

- AA drafts within ten days of execution; LA reviews; counsel confirms the accuracy of any summarized legal terms.
- One page per structure; two at most. Write for a rising-generation member with no financial training.
- File with the structure's documents in the [Document vault] and in the notebook (T08); review with the client at the 45-Day Follow-up or the next progress meeting.
- The communication plan is the client's decision; the practice drafts it, the client owns it.
