# T14 — Family map and matrices

## Purpose

The single record of who is in the family, what they hold, who decides what, what may be shared with whom, how the family communicates, and which transitions are underway. Every Mode A procedure reads from it; SOP 40 governs it.

## How to use

- CSA opens it at handoff with the known facts; LA completes Parts 1–2 after the household and family discoveries (SOP 10, SOP 41); Parts 3–5 are drafted from what the family already does, every *de facto* entry labeled, and confirmed at the first family meeting (SOP 43).
- Each adult signs their own row in Part 4 before anything about them is discussed with anyone else.
- Kept in [CRM] with a PDF in the family's [Document vault]. Reviewed at every annual family meeting and on any event in SOP 22.
