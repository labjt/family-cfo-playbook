# T27 — Onboarding plan, meeting-role menu and client introduction

## Purpose

The working forms for SOP 73: the 30/60/90-day grid, the menu of named roles a new professional takes in client meetings, the introduction wording, the second-chair register, the two promotion-test checklists, and the 90-day review.

## How to use

- LA and the new hire complete Part 1 together in the first week and update it at each weekly one-on-one.
- Every client meeting in the first quarter carries one Part 2 role on the agenda (T02).
- Parts 5 and 6 are scored relationship by relationship; a "yes" on every line moves the status in [CRM].

## Part 2 — Meeting-role menu (assign one per meeting)

| Role | What it means in the room | Typical for |
|---|---|---|
| Notes and follow-through | Records decisions; sends the recap; owns every task created | CSA, AA, SA (first month) |
| Presenter of one analysis | Presents a defined piece — performance vs plan, a cash-flow projection, a scenario | SA, AA |
| Named specialist | Introduced as the person for one topic (planning software, reporting, a technical area) | SA |
| Project owner | Owns a defined multi-week deliverable for this family (transfer, cost basis, entity map) | SA, AA, CSA |
| Second chair | Sits beside the LA on a complex relationship; contributes on analysis and decisions; not yet the client's first call | SA |

## Part 3 — Client-introduction wording

In the meeting (two sentences at most): "______ joined us as ______ and trained in ______ ; today ______ will ______ , and from now on ______ is your first call for ______ ."

In the confirmation letter (T01): "______ , our ______ , will join us on ______ and will ______ ; [Advisor] remains your point of contact for anything strategic."

## Part 5 — Relationship-manager test (all five must hold)

- [ ] Runs most regularly scheduled meetings independently
- [ ] The client calls this person first
- [ ] The client brings new plans and life changes to this person and expects service to adjust
- [ ] The client gives this person both praise and complaints
- [ ] The client asks this person for more resources or a change of service

Result: relationship-manager status recorded in [CRM] on ______ ; LA moves to quality-control role.

## Part 6 — Relationship-owner test (all four must hold)

- [ ] Conducts all meetings, including strategy and portfolio decisions
- [ ] The client seeks no other senior professional's advice
- [ ] Has brought new assets or services into the relationship
- [ ] Can delegate part of the work to others with the client's acceptance

Result: owner status (lead responsibility; the relationship belongs to the practice) recorded on ______ .
