# T05 — Engagement scoping worksheet

## Purpose

One page per engagement that records who the client is, which mode and scope apply, who may instruct, and what information may be shared with whom. It is opened at handoff, completed after Discovery, signed at Mutual Commitment, and reviewed annually or on any trigger in SOP 22.

## How to use

- CSA opens the sheet in [CRM] at business-development handoff (Part 1). LA completes Parts 2–5 after CCM1 and confirms them with the client before CCM2. Signed with the engagement letter at CCM3. Filed in the client's [Document vault].
- Every field must have an answer; "unknown" is an answer that creates a task.
