# T10 — Advanced-planning tracker

## Purpose

The single page that shows the whole advanced-planning picture — every recommended action in the four areas, its priority, the client's decision, its status, and the next step — so each Regular Progress Meeting starts from the same place and nothing is lost between meetings. It is Part 3 of the wealth management plan (SOP 30) in working form.

## How to use

- AA maintains it; LA reviews it before every RPM; CSA files each dated version to the [Document vault].
- Update within 48 hours of every RPM and every expert-network meeting. Never delete a row — move it to *dropped* with the date and reason.
- Priority is the impact on the client's life, not the size of the fee or the ease of execution. Keep no more than three items per area marked *high*.
- Status codes: **C** completed · **P** in progress · **F** future · **X** dropped. Decisions: **do** · **defer** (with a revisit date) · **drop** · **refer** (Mode B: to family team).
- Mode B: fill the scope tag on every row from T05 — *in* (Advisor owns) · *FT* (family team owns) · *co* (coordination only).
