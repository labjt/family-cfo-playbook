# T15 — Annual service calendar

## Purpose

The practice runs on a calendar, not on memory. This template lays out the standing items of a client-service year, quarter by quarter, so every client receives the same rhythm, every deadline has an owner, and the team's own operating cadence (SOP 74) is visible beside the client work.

## How to use

- CSA maintains one master calendar in [CRM] and one per-client instance (Part 2). Dates are set in December for the following year; the LA approves.
- Every item names the SOP it executes and the template it produces. Items marked **family** apply to Mode A only; items marked **B** apply per the scope rung on T05.
- Review the calendar at the quarterly team meeting (SOP 74). Move items only with the LA's agreement and record why.
