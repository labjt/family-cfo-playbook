# T13 — Scripts sheet

## Purpose

A one-place convenience copy of the practice's working scripts. The canonical version of each script lives inline in its SOP; if the two ever differ, the SOP wins. Every script is two sentences or fewer, in the practice's own words, so it can be said naturally.

## How to use

- Read the script for the meeting you are about to run; say it in your own voice, not from the page.
- The label on each script names the SOP it belongs to. Update this sheet when the SOP changes (SOP 60).
- Scripts marked *beyond Bowen* come from the family layer and are used in Mode A, or in Mode B when the family invites the conversation.

## Discovery (SOP 10)

| Moment | Script |
|---|---|
| Opening — qualify, don't sell | "I'm glad you're here; our purpose today is to find out whether we are the right firm for your family. If it turns out we are not, I'll say so and point you to someone who is." |
| Second-order follow-up | "You said financial security matters most — what specifically about it concerns you?" |
| Ask about assets fourth | "Now that I understand what matters and who matters, let's map what you own and where it sits." |
| Process preference | "How do you like to work with your advisors — how often, by what means, and with how much detail?" |
| Precommitment and next steps | "Based on what you've told me I believe we can make a real difference; the next step is for us to prepare your investment plan and meet again in two weeks. Shall we fix the date now?" |
| Mode B mandate | "Your family already has a central team — tell me what they do for you, what you want from me, and who else needs to know we are working together." |

## Investment plan meeting (SOP 12)

| Moment | Script |
|---|---|
| Commitment ask — no check today | "If this plan makes sense to you, I'd like you to take it home and live with it for a week; then we meet again and, if you're ready, we put it to work together." |
| Set the stage for advanced planning | "Investments are the foundation, not the whole house; once we're working together, my network of specialists will look at everything beyond the portfolio." |

## Mutual commitment meeting (SOP 14)

| Moment | Script |
|---|---|
| Congratulations | "Congratulations — this is an important step toward everything you told me matters to your family." |
| Buyer's-remorse and media inoculation | "It is normal to feel a flicker of doubt in the coming weeks, especially when the financial news gets loud; treat that noise as entertainment and let the plan do its work." |
| Concentrated gains, long view | "Returns arrive unevenly and the best days are impossible to time; staying invested through the dull and the frightening stretches is how the plan achieves its goals." |
| Introductions ask #1 | "We serve a small number of families like yours; who else comes to mind who might value what we're now doing for you?" |
| Mode B — into the family team | "So that we coordinate well from the start, would you introduce me to the people on your family's team who should know we are working together?" |

## 45-day follow-up (SOP 16)

| Moment | Script |
|---|---|
| Opening | "What has happened since we last met?" |
| Returns reframe | "What matters is achieving your goals over your lifetime, not the last forty-five days; in the short run nobody can predict the market, and in the long run prudent decisions are what tilt the odds in your favor." |
| Introductions ask #2 | "As we talked about last time, who else comes to mind who might benefit from the way we work?" |

## Regular progress meeting (SOP 20)

| Moment | Script |
|---|---|
| Opener | "What have you been doing since we last got together?" |
| Drill deeper | "Has anything happened in your life or the family's that affects your finances and that I should know about?" |
| Drop / defer / do | "For each item, tell me: do it, hold it for now, or take it off the list — and I'll record why." |
| Additional-assets ask | "Part of my job is to keep the whole picture in view, including the accounts we don't manage; may we look at how those fit with the plan?" |
| Second-opinion offer | "Would it help if we gave you a second opinion on how that account is being managed, at no cost and with no obligation?" |
| Introductions ask | "We work best with families like yours; who else comes to mind who might value what we do for you?" |
| Keep asking | "Who else comes to mind?" |
| Fee conversation (annual) | "Once a year I put our fees in writing, in the context of everything we do for the family, and I'd like you to lead the questions." |

## Calling an introduced prospect (SOP 50)

| Response | Script |
|---|---|
| "No — I have an advisor" | "That's exactly why families come to us: would a complimentary second opinion on your current plan be useful?" |
| "Maybe" | "There's no cost for a Discovery meeting, and we only go further if we're sure we can add real value." |
| "Yes" | "Let's set a date; I'll send a short note listing what to bring so the meeting is worth your time." |

## Family layer

!!! extension "Beyond Bowen"
    The scripts below come from the Hughes material and our own extensions; use them in Mode A, or in Mode B when the family invites the conversation.

| Moment | Script |
|---|---|
| Passion and mentor (SOP 10, 41) | "Two questions I ask everyone in a family: what are you passionate about, and who is your mentor?" |
| The children's-wealth question (SOP 41, 43) | "How wealthy do you want your children to be?" |
| The dream question (SOP 42, 43) | "What is your dream — and how can the family invest in it?" |
| The beneficiary question (SOP 46) | "May I ask the question nobody asks: are you an excellent beneficiary, and what would it take to become one?" |
| Family-meeting opener (SOP 43) | "What needs to happen in the next few hours for each of you to say this was worth the trip?" |
| Gratitude close (SOP 43) | "Before we close, each of us names one person we're grateful to this year and how we'll tell them." |
| Trustee–beneficiary success (SOP 46) | "What must happen today for you to leave thinking this was a very good meeting?" |
| Distribution conversation (SOP 46) | "Tell me what this would do to enhance your life — and if the trust didn't exist, how would you pursue the same goal?" |
| Consequences check before a perpetual structure (SOP 20, 30) | "Before we build something meant to last generations: what could this do to the people it's meant to help, and what harm might we do before we do good?" |
| Advance notice to heirs (SOP 42) | "Nobody should learn about a trust from a trustee's phone call; let's plan how and when each of your children hears about what is coming." |
