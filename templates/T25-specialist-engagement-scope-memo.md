# T25 — Specialist engagement scope memo and bench scorecard

## Purpose

One short memo per borrowed engagement — internal bench, external network member, or vendor — that fixes the deliverable, the data, the confidentiality wall and the credit before any work starts; plus the debrief that turns each engagement into a roster entry.

## How to use

- CSA opens the memo from the trigger recorded in [CRM]; LA completes and sends it to the specialist; the specialist confirms by reply. Filed in the client's [Document vault] under "advisors and specialists."
- After delivery, the LA completes the debrief and scorecard; the CSA updates the bench roster in SOP 31.
- Five lines suffice for a small matter; the wall section is mandatory whenever a sister company also serves the family.
