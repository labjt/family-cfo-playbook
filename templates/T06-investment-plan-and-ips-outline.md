# T06 — Investment plan and IPS outline

## Purpose

The fill-in skeleton for every investment plan produced under SOP 13. Part 1 is the client-facing executive summary; Part 2 is the governing investment policy statement; the addendum table handles families with several entities and trusts.

## How to use

- AA copies this outline into [Planning software] or the firm's document template and completes every section; blanks are `______`.
- LA edits Part 1 for voice; peer reviewer signs the block at the end; [Firm Compliance] initials the fee and disclosure pages.
- File the final in the [Document vault] under *Investment Plan*; put the one-page summary at the front of the notebook (T08).
