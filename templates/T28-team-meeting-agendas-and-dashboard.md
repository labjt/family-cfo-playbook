# T28 — Team meeting agendas, dashboard, capacity check and bogey worksheet

## Purpose

The forms behind SOP 74 and SOP 70: standing agendas for each internal meeting, the five-ratio dashboard with target bands, the capacity-check worksheet that reads the hiring triggers, the founder's bogey worksheet that sets the hiring budget, the per-professional productivity table, and the annual porch-of-indecision review.

## How to use

- CSA prepares the agendas the day before each meeting and the dashboard before each monthly and quarterly review.
- LA completes the bogey worksheet privately once a year and whenever a hiring decision is on the table; only the resulting surplus figure enters the capacity check.
- Fill the tables in place; keep prior quarters for trend.

## Part 1 — Standing agendas

**Weekly team meeting (30 min)**
1. Next two weeks of client meetings — attendee, named role, packet status, anything the client must hear beforehand.
2. Open service items — who has the ball; anything older than a week.
3. Red flags raised since last week (SOP 22) and the contact made.
4. Implementation tracker exceptions (T07).
5. Borrowed engagements due this week (T25).
6. Decisions and owners.

**Monthly principal review (45 min)**
1. EBOC and overhead % vs bands.
2. Service-failure log — each entry mapped to an SOP step.
3. Borrowed-expertise pipeline and scorecards.
4. Hours by professional; borrowed hours.
5. Actions from last month.

**Quarterly review (2 h)**
1. Common-sized P&L vs 40 / 35 / 25.
2. Capacity check (Part 3) vs SOP 70 triggers.
3. SOP review (SOP 60): changed, failed, missing.
4. Client review: fit, risk, unpriced customization.
5. Productivity measures (Part 5).
6. State of the team — what is working, what is not, what each person needs.
7. Decisions and dates.

**Annual (half day, Q4)**
1. Budget by cost owner, loaded for monthly variance review.
2. Performance reviews (standards stated) → compensation decisions.
3. Equity and succession conversation.
4. Porch-of-indecision review (Part 6).
5. Roadmap block in SOP 70 updated.
