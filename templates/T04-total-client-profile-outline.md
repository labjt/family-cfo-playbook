# T04 — Total Client Profile outline

## Purpose

The typed counterpart of the mind map: a nested outline the AA fills within 24 hours of Discovery and maintains in [CRM]. Level 1 is the family (Mode A only); level 2 is one outline per household. Every node carries a confidentiality flag: **S** shared at family level · **H** household only · **P** private to the individual.

## How to use

- Transcribe from the mind-map scan in the client's own words; put interpretation in the notes column, not the node.
- Date every node when confirmed; blank means "not yet discussed" — record that explicitly.
- Set flags from T05 (Mode B) or T14 (Mode A) before sharing any extract with a specialist.
