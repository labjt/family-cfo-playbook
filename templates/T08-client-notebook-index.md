# T08 — Client notebook index

## Purpose

The table of contents for the client's notebook — the [Document vault] structure (and the physical binder where a client wants one) that holds every plan, policy, statement, and communication for the life of the relationship. It is built at the 45-Day Follow-up and maintained by the CSA thereafter.

## How to use

- Create the folder structure below in the [Document vault] the day of CCM3; populate during implementation; walk it with the client at CCM4.
- Every document filed gets a row in the register. Every progress meeting adds its report and recap.
- Access permissions mirror the consent on T05 (Mode B) or the confidentiality matrix on T14 (Mode A); review permissions whenever consent changes.
- Retain at least five years of material in the live notebook; archive older years per [Firm] policy without deleting.

## Sections

| # | Section | Sub-sections | What goes here |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | Start here | One-page summary; contact card ("who you call for what"); this index | The first page anyone opens |
| 1 | Investment plan | Executive summary (current); superseded versions | The plan presented at CCM2 and later revisions |
| 2 | Investment policy statement | Master IPS; per-entity / per-trust addenda; annual review log | The governing policy and its history |
| 3 | Wealth management plan | Advanced-planning plan and tracker (T10); structure summaries (T21); expert-network recommendations | Built from the first progress meeting onward |
| 4 | Agreements | Engagement letter; T05 / T14; fee schedule; disclosures; conflicts acknowledgements | Everything signed with the practice |
| 5 | Accounts | Per account/entity/trust: opening documents, executed forms, custodian confirmations, T07 | Implementation record |
| 6 | Statements | Custodian statements by account and quarter | Filed as received |
| 7 | Progress reports | Quarterly reporting packages (T11); meeting recaps | The relationship's running record |
| 8 | Family (Mode A) | Family map (T14); family meeting packs (T16); executive summary (T17); capitals assessment (T18) | Family-level material, permissioned separately |
| 9 | Trusts | Per trust: instrument summary, IPS addendum, trustee correspondence, meeting agendas (T22), distribution requests and memos (T23) | Trustee–beneficiary record |
| 10 | Newsletters and firm communications | Firm updates; personnel changes; event-driven notes | What the practice sent |
| 11 | Miscellaneous | Reading guides for document types; correspondence; anything uncategorized | Keep small; re-file when a category emerges |
