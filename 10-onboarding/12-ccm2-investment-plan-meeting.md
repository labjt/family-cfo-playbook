# 12 — CCM2 Investment Plan Meeting

The second consultative meeting presents a written investment plan built from the Total Client Profile. It confirms the client's goals in writing, demonstrates the thoroughness of the practice, and asks for a commitment to proceed — but deliberately stops short of taking money. The client takes the plan home and returns a week later for the Mutual Commitment Meeting.

## Purpose

- Present a detailed, actionable investment plan and investment policy statement that maximize the probability of achieving what the client said matters.
- Clarify and put in writing the goals surfaced at Discovery so both sides are aligned before anything is implemented.
- Position the Advisor as the expert who addresses wealth preservation first, with advanced planning to follow through the expert network.
- Obtain a commitment to move forward, without executing anything at this meeting.

## Trigger

CCM1 (or the Rediscovery variant) is complete, the client fits the practice, and T05 mode and scope are confirmed. This meeting is scheduled in the room at the end of Discovery.

## Roles

Owner: LA · Does: AA (drafts the plan per SOP 13; presents one defined analysis in the room), CSA (letter, packet, confirmation, [CRM]) · Consulted: peer reviewer (a second professional — SA, or an ISB colleague — signs off on the plan before it is presented); ISB / [Firm] investment platform for portfolio construction.

## Timing

- **+2 weeks after CCM1.** The plan is drafted by day 7, peer-reviewed by day 10, packet complete by day 11.
- Meeting length: 60–75 minutes.
- CCM3 is scheduled in the room, **+1 week** after this meeting.

## Inputs

- Total Client Profile (SOP 11) and the Discovery recording/notes.
- T05 — mode, scope rung, who may instruct, what may be shared.
- Copies of statements and tax returns gathered at Discovery; held-away asset list.
- Firm investment philosophy, model portfolios, index and hypothetical-portfolio summaries from the [Firm] platform.
- Family-level constraints (Mode A entity/trust map; Mode B family policy or trust-instrument terms).

## Prep checklist

- [ ] Investment plan and IPS drafted to the SOP 13 spec using T06; scope banner completed.
- [ ] Peer review done and signed on the T06 sign-off block; every figure reconciled to source statements.
- [ ] Fee schedule and firm background sections final; [Firm Compliance] disclosures attached.
- [ ] Executive summary opens with the client's own words from Discovery (values, goals, relationships) — not with markets.
- [ ] The AA's named analysis chosen (for example: how the recommended allocation behaves against the client's stated goals across horizons).
- [ ] Two bound or vault-ready copies of the plan; the client's copy is the one that goes home.
- [ ] Agenda from T02 (CCM2) sent with the pre-meeting letter (T01); SOP 05 prep complete.
- [ ] The rest of the process (CCM3, CCM4, first progress meeting, expert-network cycle) ready to explain in one minute.

## Procedure

1. **Greet and reinforce.** Every team member greets the client by name. The LA reconnects with something the client said mattered at Discovery before any business (SOP 05, SOP 04).
2. **Set the stage.** State that today's purpose is to show the client a written road map built from what they told us, and to find out whether it is the right one.
3. **Walk the agenda and the road ahead.** Name the remaining steps: this meeting; the Mutual Commitment Meeting in one week; the 45-Day Follow-up; regular progress meetings at the client's chosen cadence; the expert-network cycle that produces the wealth management plan.
4. **Present the executive summary first.** Read back the seven areas of the profile as the client's own words. Ask whether anything is wrong or missing — corrections here are the point of the meeting.

    !!! script "Say something like"
        "Before we get to a single number, I want to make sure we heard you correctly — this page is what you told us matters. What would you change?"

5. **Present the recommendations in detail.** Go through the plan section by section: goals in writing, the diagnostic of the current situation, the recommended structure, how it maps to each goal, what it costs. Pause for questions after each section. The AA presents the analysis assigned in prep and answers questions on it.
6. **Introduce the IPS without walking through it.** Explain what it is (the discipline that governs how we manage money and how decisions get made), where it sits in the notebook, and that it is reviewed annually. Do not read it page by page; most clients want to know it exists, not its detail.
7. **Ask for the commitment — not the check.** Ask whether the client is ready to move forward on this basis. If yes, say plainly that we do not execute anything today: the client takes the materials home, thinks it through, and we meet in a week to make the commitment mutual.

    !!! script "Say something like"
        "If this is right, the next step is a short meeting next week where we both commit and sign. I'd rather you take this home and sleep on it than sign anything today."

8. **Solicit and address concerns.** Ask directly what worries the client about the plan, the fees, or the process. Record every concern in the notes; answer what can be answered, and commit to a written answer within 48 hours for the rest.
9. **Set the stage for the wealth management plan.** Explain that investments are the foundation and only the first layer; once the client is on board, the expert network will look at tax, transfer, protection, and charitable questions and the first progress meeting will present that plan.
10. **Close and schedule.** Summarize the decision and any open concerns. Schedule CCM3 in the room, one week out. Send the client home with their copy of the plan and a one-line note of what to look at first.
11. **Confirm in writing within 24 hours (T01).** Date and time of CCM3, the list of documents that will be executed there, and written answers to any concerns raised.

## Follow-up

- Within 24 hours: confirmation and recap sent; concerns answered or dated; [CRM] updated; CCM3 paperwork list handed to the CSA (SOP 14 prep).
- Within 48 hours: any plan corrections issued as a revised executive summary, marked as such.
- The one-week gap is deliberate; do not shorten it because the client is eager.

## Do / Don't

**Do**

- Present a plan that could only have been written for this client — it should read like Discovery, not like a brochure.
- Insist the client take the materials home even when they are ready to sign.
- Give the AA a real part; promote their analysis as their work.
- Tell the client what the plan does *not* cover (scope banner) before they ask.

**Don't**

- Don't take a check, a transfer form, or a signed agreement at this meeting.
- Don't present a "financial plan" — advanced planning comes later through the network; claiming expertise in everything is not credible.
- Don't walk through the IPS line by line.
- Don't let the platform's model names replace the client's goals as the organizing logic of the presentation.

## Mode A / Mode B

### Mode A — whole family

- The plan covers every household and entity in scope on T05; the executive summary carries a family-level page and a household-level page for each household.
- Where the family's wealth sits in several entities and trusts, present the investor-allocation overlay as a question for the family, not a done deal (SOP 13, SOP 47).
- Attendance follows the family map (T14); confidentiality between members is checked before household figures are shown in a joint meeting.

### Mode B — individual within a larger team

- Obtain the family-level investment policy or the governing trust instrument before drafting; the plan is consistent with it or carves out explicitly.
- The executive summary opens with the scope banner: what this plan covers and what the family central team owns.
- FCT review of the draft happens only if the mandate on T05 says so; otherwise the FCT receives a courtesy summary per consent.
- Under B-narrow, the "plan" is the mandate-specific IPS and a one-page summary; keep the meeting to 45 minutes.

## Metrics

- Share of CCM2 meetings held within 10–18 days of CCM1.
- Share of plans peer-signed before presentation (target 100%).
- Share of CCM2 meetings that ended with CCM3 scheduled (target 100%).
