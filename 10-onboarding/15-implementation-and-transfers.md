# 15 — Implementation and transfers

Bowen's process jumps from the signatures at the Mutual Commitment Meeting to the notebook at the 45-Day Follow-up. In a multi-entity, multi-custodian family the six weeks in between are where administrative errors happen — and administrative error is the fastest way to lose a client who has just said yes. This procedure makes that window a managed project owned by the CSA, with the Lead Advisor accountable to the client.

## Purpose

- Move every account, entity, and trust to its target structure accurately, on time, and with no surprises.
- Keep the client informed at a steady cadence so the transfer period feels like service, not silence.
- Produce a plain-language summary and communication plan for every new structure so the family understands what was created and what to tell whom.
- Arrive at the 45-Day Follow-up with everything collected, filed, and explainable.

## Trigger

Documents executed at CCM3 (SOP 14). Also triggered later by any new account, new trust or entity, change of custodian, or restructuring decided at a progress meeting (SOP 20).

## Roles

Owner: CSA · Does: CSA (forms, submissions, tracker, custodian follow-up, client updates), AA (in-kind vs liquidate analysis, cost-basis reconciliation, structure summaries), LA (decisions on transition method, client escalation calls, sign-off) · Consulted: [Trust Co.] and trustees for trust accounts; [Firm Compliance] for titling and beneficiary rules; ISB (tax, insurance) where a structure changes ownership; FCT or family custodian in Mode B.

## Timing

| Day | Milestone |
|---|---|
| Day 0 (CCM3) | Forms executed; T07 opened for every account |
| Day 1 | All forms two-person checked and submitted; client sent the "what happens next" note |
| Days 2–10 | Custodian confirmations; account numbers received; transfers initiated |
| Days 5–25 | Assets arrive; cost basis collected; positions reconciled; trades per the transition plan |
| Weekly | Client status update (or per the client's process preference) |
| Day 30 | Reconciliation complete; structure summaries (T21) drafted; notebook (T08) populated |
| Day 40 | Pre-meeting packet for CCM4 assembled; exceptions closed or scheduled |
| Day ~45 | CCM4 (SOP 16) |

## Inputs

- Executed forms and the paperwork list from CCM3; T05 (who may instruct, consent).
- Investment plan recommendations and transition notes (SOP 13): which positions move in kind, which are sold, when.
- Entity and trust documents: trust instruments, trustee acceptance, entity resolutions, EINs.
- Current custodian statements for every account moving; contact details of the delivering firms.
- Beneficiary designations on retirement accounts and insurance; current ownership of policies.

## Prep checklist

- [ ] T07 rows created for every account, entity, and trust, with the target custodian, action, and owner.
- [ ] Two-person check protocol assigned (preparer and checker named per form).
- [ ] Transition plan from the AA: for each position, in kind / liquidate / hold, with the tax reason and the LA's initials.
- [ ] Trust accounts: trustee signatures and, where [Trust Co.] is trustee, the SOP 71 scope memo in place.
- [ ] Client's preferred update channel and frequency taken from the Total Client Profile.
- [ ] Notebook (T08) skeleton created in the [Document vault] with permissions matching T05/T14.

## Procedure

1. **Open the tracker.** Enter every account on T07 the same day as CCM3. Nothing moves that is not on the tracker.
2. **Two-person check every form.** Preparer completes; checker verifies names, registrations, account numbers, signatures, dates, and attachments against the plan. Log the check on T07. Submit only after both initials.
3. **Submit and confirm.** Send account openings and transfer requests; record submission dates and expected completion. Chase any custodian acknowledgement not received within two business days.
4. **Send the "what happens next" note (T01).** Within 24 hours of CCM3: which accounts are opening, roughly when assets will land, what mail or portal messages the client will receive from custodians, and that the practice — not the client — will act on them.
5. **Initiate transfers per the transition plan.** Move in-kind positions first; execute liquidations and purchases on the LA's instruction and the platform's process; record every trade decision and its reason on T07.
6. **Collect cost basis.** Request cost basis from delivering firms at submission; reconcile against arriving positions; escalate missing lots at day 15. Do not let the first progress report go out with unknown basis.
7. **Complete titling and designations.** Retitle trust and entity accounts exactly as the instruments name them; update beneficiary designations on retirement accounts and life insurance to match the plan; obtain entity resolutions where custodians require them; change insurance ownership only with the ISB insurance specialist's confirmation and counsel's sign-off.
8. **Review the tracker daily.** CSA reviews every open row each morning: status, next action, owner, date. Anything past its expected date becomes an exception (step 11).
9. **Update the client at the agreed cadence.** Weekly by default, or per the process preference: what has arrived, what is pending, what the client need do (usually nothing). Silence during transfers is a service failure.
10. **Draft a structure summary (T21) for every new trust, entity, gift, or plan.** The AA drafts, the LA reviews, counsel confirms accuracy where a legal document is summarized. Include the communication plan: what the client should tell whom, when, and how.
11. **Handle exceptions the same day.** A delayed custodian, a rejected form, a missing signature: log it, tell the client before they notice, fix it, and record the fix (SOP 23). The person who found the problem owns it to resolution.
12. **Reconcile and close.** At day 30, reconcile every account to the plan: positions, cash, basis, registrations, beneficiaries. Populate the notebook (T08) with account confirmations, executed documents, the plan, and the IPS. Hand the CCM4 packet to the LA at day 40.

## Follow-up

- Day 1: submissions confirmed; client note sent.
- Weekly: status update sent; T07 reviewed with the LA in the team meeting.
- Day 30: reconciliation signed by the AA; T21 summaries reviewed by the LA.
- Day 40: exceptions closed or scheduled with the client's knowledge; CCM4 agenda (T02) drafted from the tracker.

## Do / Don't

**Do**

- Treat every form as a two-person job; the checker is not a formality.
- Tell the client about a delay before the custodian's letter does.
- Save every confirmation, every notice, and every executed document — the notebook is built from them.
- Write the structure summary in words a rising-generation member could understand.

**Don't**

- Don't submit a form the same person prepared without a second check.
- Don't liquidate a position without the LA's initials on the transition plan.
- Don't leave beneficiary designations for "later"; a mismatch between the plan and the designation is a plan failure.
- Don't let a specialist, custodian, or sister company become the client's contact for status — the CSA owns communication.

## Mode A / Mode B

### Mode A — whole family

- Track by household and entity; the family-level summary of what moved and what was created goes to the family's designated contact per T14.
- Trust accounts follow the trustee's instructions; where [Trust Co.] is trustee, it acts under the SOP 71 scope memo and the CSA still owns client communication.
- T21 summaries are the raw material for the family's staged-disclosure conversation (SOP 43); do not distribute them to members until the principal has decided the communication plan.

### Mode B — individual within a larger team

- Obtain FCT or trustee approvals before initiating any transfer that touches family-controlled accounts; record the approval on T07.
- The custodian may be the family's; adapt forms and timelines to their process, and agree a single point of contact there (SOP 33).
- Copy transfer status to the FCT only per the consent on T05; the individual receives every update regardless.
- Under B-narrow, the tracker covers the mandated accounts only; do not collect information about accounts outside the mandate.

## Metrics

- Not-in-good-order (returned) forms as a share of forms submitted (target < 2%).
- Accounts fully reconciled — positions, basis, registration, beneficiaries — by day 30 (target 100%).
- Client updates sent on schedule during the transfer window (target 100%).
