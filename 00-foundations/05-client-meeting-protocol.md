# 05 — Client meeting protocol

Every client meeting in this practice — the five consultative meetings, family meetings, trustee meetings, and ad hoc reviews — runs inside this wrapper. The meeting-specific SOP supplies the body; this procedure supplies the preparation, the opening, the close, and the follow-up so that the client has the same experience every time, whoever is in the room.

## Purpose

- Deliver a consistent, prepared, agenda-driven experience that signals the practice is a refined process, not an individual's improvisation.
- Make sure every meeting ends with the next one scheduled and every decision recorded within 24 hours.
- Let team members and borrowed specialists take part without the client ever feeling "passed down."

## Trigger

Any client-facing meeting is scheduled — in person, video, or phone — with a client, a prospective client, a family group, or a trustee/beneficiary.

## Roles

Owner: LA · Does: CSA (letter, confirmation, logistics, packet assembly, [CRM] entries), AA (analysis in the packet; a named role in the room) · Consulted: ISB / EEN specialists when they attend.

## Timing

| When | What |
|---|---|
| T−10 business days | Meeting scheduled in [CRM]; agenda drafted from T02 |
| T−7 | Pre-meeting letter or email sent (T01) with the agenda and anything the client should bring |
| T−3 | Packet assembled; team briefing held; any change since the last meeting the client should hear *before* the meeting is communicated now |
| T−1 | Confirmation call or message |
| T+0 | Meeting |
| T+1 (≤ 24 hours) | Written recap, confirmation of the next meeting, [CRM] log, tasks assigned |
| T+7 | One-week call after a first meeting or any meeting with heavy disclosures or decisions |

## Inputs

- The meeting-specific agenda (T02) and the SOP for that meeting.
- The client's Total Client Profile (SOP 11) and the recap of the last meeting.
- Open items: implementation tracker (T07), advanced-planning tracker (T10), service-failure log.
- The most recent quarter-end statements or reporting package (SOP 24).
- The client's stated process preference (how they want to be contacted and how often) and personality notes (SOP 04).
- T05 for who may attend and what may be discussed in front of whom.

## Prep checklist

- [ ] Agenda adapted from T02; client invited to add items in the pre-meeting letter.
- [ ] Packet: agenda, prior-quarter figures, tracker extracts, any documents to sign with "sign here" flags.
- [ ] Attendee list checked against T05 (consent) and the client's wishes; every team member or specialist attending has a **named role** for this meeting.
- [ ] Anything material that changed since the last contact has already been told to the client — no surprises in the room.
- [ ] Room or video link tested; beverages; no financial television or market commentary visible in the waiting area.
- [ ] Recording plan decided (Discovery and family-executive-summary interviews are recorded with consent; otherwise notes by the AA/CSA).
- [ ] Personality note reviewed: how this client likes to be greeted, paced, and presented to.

## Procedure

1. **Send the pre-meeting letter (T01).** Confirm date, time, place, purpose, attendees, and what to bring. Attach the agenda and invite additions.
2. **Brief the team.** Two minutes per attendee: what they will do in the room, what they may and may not discuss, who takes notes.
3. **Greet.** Every team member greets the client by name, formally. The LA reinforces something the client values (a recent event, an interest) before any business.
4. **Introduce anyone new** — by name, role in the practice, and what they will do for this client today. Promote their skills; never sound uncertain about them.
5. **Open with purpose and success.** State why we are meeting. Ask what needs to happen for the client to leave feeling this was time well spent. Walk the agenda and ask for additions.

    !!! script "Say something like"
        "Before we start: what would make this a genuinely useful hour for you? I'll make sure we get there."

6. **Ask what has changed.** "What has happened since we last met?" — then, if needed, "Anything in your life or the family's that affects your finances and that I should know about?" Listen for marriages, births, deaths, health, job or business changes, sales, disputes.
7. **Run the body** per the meeting-specific SOP. Present, then pause for questions; do not answer everything immediately if the packet will answer it in sequence.
8. **Coach decisions, don't push.** For each recommendation, the client chooses drop / defer / do (Mode B adds *refer to family team*). Record the choice.
9. **Close.** Summarize decisions and next steps by owner and date. Ask whether the meeting achieved what the client wanted and what to do differently next time.
10. **Schedule the next meeting in the room** according to the client's stated cadence. Never leave with "we'll be in touch."
11. **Confirm in writing within 24 hours (T01).** Date and time of the next meeting, decisions, tasks with owners, and any documents outstanding.
12. **Log in [CRM] within 24 hours.** Life changes, decisions, asks made and outcomes, the personality note, and the next-meeting date.

## Follow-up

- Within 24 hours: recap and confirmation sent; tasks created; documents filed to the [Document vault]; tracker(s) updated.
- Within one week: after a first meeting or any meeting with weighty disclosures, the LA calls to answer questions that surfaced on reflection.
- Any promise made in the room has a date and an owner before the day ends.

## Outputs and records

| Output | Where it lives | Owner |
|---|---|---|
| Agenda and pre-meeting letter | [CRM] activity; client's [Document vault] | CSA |
| Meeting notes and recap | [CRM]; recap sent to the client | AA / CSA |
| Decisions and tasks | [CRM] tasks; T07 / T10 trackers | CSA |
| Next-meeting confirmation | [CRM] calendar; written confirmation | CSA |
| Updated personality and process notes | [CRM] client record | LA |

## Do / Don't

**Do**

- Prepare an agenda for every meeting, including the "quick" ones.
- Tell the client bad news before the meeting, never in it.
- Keep Discovery to ninety minutes; keep every other meeting inside the time promised.
- Give every junior in the room something substantive to do — the notes and follow-through, one analysis to present, one project to own.

**Don't**

- Don't take the check at the investment-plan meeting; commitment has its own meeting.
- Don't let a specialist become the client's point of contact by default.
- Don't answer the first question with a lecture; the packet and the sequence usually answer it.
- Don't "pass the puck" — whoever receives a request in the meeting owns it to completion.

## Mode A / Mode B

### Mode A — whole family

- Attendance follows the family map and communication map (T14); confidentiality between members is checked before anyone joins.
- Family meetings add the ground rules and facilitation protocol in SOP 43 on top of this wrapper.

### Mode B — individual within a larger team

- Recap distribution follows the consent on T05: the individual only, unless the FCT is authorized.
- Recording consent explicitly covers privacy from the family and the FCT, not only from third parties.
- If an FCT representative attends, the LA states at the opening what will and will not be covered in their presence.

## Metrics

- Share of meetings with a written confirmation sent within 24 hours (target 100%).
- Share of meetings that ended with the next meeting scheduled (target 100%).
- Service-failure log entries traceable to a meeting promise (target zero).
