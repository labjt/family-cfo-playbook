# 02 — Engagement modes

The practice serves families in two fundamentally different configurations. Every procedure in this library branches on which one applies, so the mode — and, for Mode B, the scope rung — is decided at intake, written on T05, confirmed in the engagement letter, and revisited whenever the family's situation changes.

## Purpose

- Define Mode A and Mode B and the three Mode B scope rungs.
- Fix the rules for **who the client is** and **what may be shared with whom**.
- Hold the master matrix of what changes in each SOP under Mode B, so the Advisor can see every delta in one place.

## The two modes

### Mode A — whole family under the Advisor's umbrella

The Advisor is the family's personal chief financial officer. The family (one or several households, their entities and trusts) has no other central advisory team; the Advisor's expert network, the firm's internal bench, and the family's incumbent CPA/attorney are coordinated by the Advisor. The family layer (SOPs 40–48) applies in full.

### Mode B — individuals inside a family that has its own central team

A large family ($100M+) has a family office, trust company, counsel, or lead advisor of its own — the **Family Central Team (FCT)**. The Advisor serves one or more individuals or branches of that family. The FCT quarterbacks family-level structures; the Advisor's role is scoped by mandate.

| Scope rung | What the Advisor owns | What the FCT owns | Typical use |
|---|---|---|---|
| **B-full** | Full wealth management for the individual/branch: investment consulting, advanced planning for that person's own assets, relationship management | Family-level structures (dynasty trusts, family entities, family-wide investment policy, governance) | A G2 sibling with her own household, business interest, and personal assets |
| **B-partial** | Investment consulting for the individual's assets plus liaison into the FCT's planning process; contributes into their advanced planning rather than running his own | Advanced planning, tax, estate for the whole family including this individual | A family member whose family office does all planning centrally |
| **B-narrow** | A defined mandate only — one trust's portfolio, one member's personal accounts | Everything else | A trustee appoints the Advisor to manage one trust |

## Who is the client — rules

1. **Name the client(s) in writing** on T05 and in the engagement letter: the individuals and, in Mode A, the households and entities. "The family" is never the client without a named decision-maker for each entity.
2. **Answer the three questions** at intake and annually: who pays the fee; who is the client; who advocates for the rising generation. Record the answers on T05.
3. **Mode A with governance in place:** when a family council or equivalent exists, it is the Advisor's institutional counterparty for family-level matters; individual households remain clients in their own right.
4. **Mode B:** the individual is the client. The FCT is a counterparty to coordinate with under SOP 33, never a party that can instruct the Advisor about that individual's affairs unless the individual has authorized it on T05.
5. **A couple** is one client unit with two people who may each speak directly with the Advisor (SOP 21); either spouse may hold information in confidence from the other only where the engagement letter says so and [Firm Compliance] has approved.

## Default confidentiality rule (both modes)

An adult's personal information is not shared upward (to parents), sideways (to siblings or spouses of siblings), or with a family's central team without that adult's written consent recorded on T05 (Mode B) or T14 (Mode A). Consent is specific — *what* may be shared, *with whom*, *for what purpose* — and is reviewed annually. Where a sister company of [Firm] also sits on the family's central team, the same wall applies inside the firm.

## Engagement scoping — the procedure in brief

1. **At handoff from business development** the CSA opens T05 with the known facts (source, relationship, family context, known advisors, conflicts check).
2. **During and after CCM1** the LA completes the mode determination and, for Mode B, proposes the scope rung and the information-sharing consent.
3. **Before CCM2** the LA confirms the mandate with the client (and, in Mode B, whether the FCT needs a courtesy notice) so the investment plan is scoped correctly.
4. **At CCM3** the engagement letter states the client, the mode, the scope, the fee basis, and the consent; T05 is signed alongside it.
5. **Annually** (at the first progress meeting of the year) and **on any trigger** in SOP 22, the LA reviews T05 and re-scopes.

## When modes change

- A Mode B individual's family later asks the Advisor to serve the whole family → re-scope to Mode A; run SOP 41 (family discovery) before expanding the plan.
- A Mode A family sets up its own family office or engages a central advisor → decide, family by family, whether the Advisor becomes the FCT or moves to Mode B for the households he continues to serve; update T05 for every affected client.
- A member of a Mode A family asks for confidential advice the rest of the family must not see → serve that member under the confidentiality rule; if the member's interests conflict with the family's, disclose the conflict and, if necessary, arrange a separate advisor for that member (SOP 40).

What changes under Mode B, SOP by SOP. Each SOP carries the detail in its own `## Mode A / Mode B` section; this table is the overview.

| 11 Total Client Profile | Family-context node (position, entities with an interest but no control, information the individual cannot access) and an FCT table; per-node confidentiality flag |
| 12 / 13 Investment plan and IPS | Obtain family-level IPS or trust-instrument constraints first; the IPS is consistent with or explicitly carved out from family policy; a scope banner states what is and is not covered; FCT review only if the mandate says so |

| 15 Implementation | Transfers may need FCT or trustee approval; status copied to the FCT per consent; the custodian may be the family's |
