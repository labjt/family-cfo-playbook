# 24 — Quarterly reporting package

Every client receives a written picture of where they stand each quarter, whether or not a meeting happens that quarter. The package consolidates every account, entity and trust we serve, measures progress against the client's own objectives rather than against last quarter's index, shows what we did and what comes next, and states plainly what they paid. It is the backbone of the notebook and the pre-read for every progress meeting.

## Purpose

- Give the client a single, consolidated view across custodians, entities and trusts.
- Measure against the investment policy statement's objectives and the plan's milestones, reinforcing the long-term perspective.
- Report transparently on fees paid to everyone.
- Carry the advanced-planning tracker and implementation status so the meeting can be about decisions.
- Produce it on a fixed schedule so the client is never waiting for information.

## Trigger

- Quarter-end statements are available from [Custodian] and any other custodians (typically 10–15 business days after quarter end).
- A year-end version adds an annual summary.
- An interim package is produced on request or after a major event (SOP 22).

## Roles

Owner: LA · Does: CSA (data gathering, assembly, delivery, filing), AA (analysis, performance vs objectives, tracker extract, draft cover summary) · Consulted: SA for the plan status; ISB ([Trust Co.]) for trust accounting extracts; FCT contact in Mode B for format and data feeds.

## Timing

| Business day after quarter end | Step |
|---|---|
| +1 to +10 | Statements and data gathered; outside-asset values requested where the client has authorized us to track them |
| +11 to +15 | AA analysis; draft package built on T11 |
| +16 to +18 | LA review and sign-off; cover summary finalized |
| +18 to +20 | Delivered per the contact plan; filed to the notebook; agenda for the next RPM updated |
| Year-end | Same clock plus the annual summary; delivered before the first progress meeting of the year |

## Inputs

- Quarter-end statements from every custodian; trust accounting extracts from [Trust Co.] where applicable.
- The investment policy statement and plan objectives (SOP 13).
- The advanced-planning tracker (T10) and implementation tracker (T07).
- The annual service calendar (T15) for upcoming items.
- Fee records: our fee, custodian and platform fees, third-party manager fees, trustee fees, any fee-sharing disclosed under SOP 31.
- The contact plan (SOP 21) — format, channel, and who receives what under T05.

## Prep checklist

- [ ] Account list reconciled against the engagement: every account, entity and trust in scope is present; nothing out of scope is included.
- [ ] Outside assets: only those the client has authorized us to track, with a note that values are client-supplied and as-of dates.
- [ ] Any customization (a special format, an extra schedule, manual outside-asset tracking) is on the priced-customization list from T05 or has been declined — nothing absorbed silently.
- [ ] Tracker extracts current: T10 statuses coded; T07 open items dated.
- [ ] Fee schedule and the quarter's actual fees reconciled.
- [ ] Anything in the package that will surprise the client has already been told to them by the LA (SOP 22).

## Procedure

1. **Gather.** CSA collects statements and data feeds from every custodian, trust extracts from [Trust Co.], and authorized outside-asset values. Missing items are chased on day +5; the package never waits past day +15 for a straggler — it goes out with a clearly marked gap and a follow-up date.
2. **Reconcile.** CSA reconciles holdings and cash across sources; discrepancies go to the AA the same day and, if unresolved by day +12, to the LA.
3. **Analyze.** AA produces performance against the IPS objectives (the client's required return, the spending or distribution rate, the risk band), the allocation against policy ranges, and a plain-language note on what drove the quarter. Short-period returns are shown but framed: what matters is progress toward the objectives over the horizon the plan set, not any single quarter.
4. **Report fees.** Every fee the client paid this quarter, by payee, in dollars and as a rate: ours, custodian and platform, third-party managers, trustee, any disclosed fee-sharing. The client should never have to ask what they paid.
5. **Pull the trackers.** T10 extract: each advanced-planning item with its status (done / in progress / scheduled / deferred / dropped) and next step. T07 extract: open transfers, titling, beneficiary designations.
6. **Write the cover summary** (AA drafts, LA rewrites): three short blocks — what changed, what we did, what is next — plus any decision the client will be asked to make at the next meeting.
7. **Assemble on T11.** Order: cover summary; consolidated holdings by entity and trust; performance vs objectives; fees; tracker extract; implementation status; upcoming calendar; questions for the next meeting.
8. **LA review.** Accuracy, tone, and whether the summary would make sense to a family member who was not in the last meeting. Sign off by day +18.
9. **Deliver** per the contact plan: [Document vault] posting with a personal note, or printed and mailed where the client prefers; a copy to each authorized recipient on T05. Trustees and beneficiaries receive the trust sections as the trust's protocol requires (SOP 46).
10. **File** to the notebook (T08) and [CRM]; update the next RPM agenda (T02) with the questions raised.
11. **Flag customization.** Any request for a new format, extra schedule or manual tracking is logged; the LA decides at the next fee conversation whether to price it or decline it (SOP 14).

## Follow-up

- Day +20: delivery confirmed in [CRM]; next-meeting agenda updated.
- Within one week of delivery: CSA checks that the client received and can open the package (a call for clients who prefer print).
- At the next RPM: the package is the pre-read; questions from it lead the agenda after the life-changes question.

## Do / Don't

**Do**

- Report against the client's objectives first; indices are context, not the scorecard.
- Show every fee, including the ones we do not receive.
- Write the cover summary in the client's language, not the platform's.
- Deliver on the same day each quarter so the client can plan around it.

**Don't**

- Don't hold the package for a late custodian; ship with a marked gap.
- Don't include accounts outside the mandate, even when the client mentions them.
- Don't let a manual outside-asset schedule become an unpriced standing job.
- Don't let the package be the first place a client sees bad news.

## Mode A / Mode B

### Mode A — whole family

- One consolidated family package at the family level (entities, trusts, households as the consent matrix allows) plus a household package per household; each household sees only what T14 authorizes.
- The family-level tracker (family goals, family-level planning items) is a separate section; the year-end version feeds the family balance sheet and income statement inputs (SOP 44).
- Trust sections go to trustees and beneficiaries per the trust protocol.

### Mode B — individual within a larger team

- Format compatible with the family's consolidated reporting; where the FCT provides a template or data specification, use it, and supply data feeds if the mandate requires.
- Only mandated accounts appear; family-level assets are referenced by name only if the client asks and the FCT agrees.
- Copies to the FCT follow the T05 consent; fee reporting covers our fees and any fee-sharing disclosed to the individual and, where the FCT gatekeeps, to the FCT.

## Metrics

- Packages delivered by business day +20 (target 100%).
- Reconciliation discrepancies unresolved at delivery (target zero).
- Customizations absorbed without pricing or a decline decision (target zero).
