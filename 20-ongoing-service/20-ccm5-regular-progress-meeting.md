# 20 — CCM5 Regular Progress Meeting

The Regular Progress Meeting is the engine of the relationship. The first one, ninety days after the follow-up meeting, presents the advanced-planning plan; every one after that reviews progress against the investment plan and the advanced-planning tracker, captures what has changed in the client's life, and is the moment for the additional-assets and introductions asks. It runs inside the meeting protocol in SOP 05.

## Purpose

- Review the client's progress toward the goals recorded in the Total Client Profile and answer questions, especially in difficult markets.
- Deepen the relationship: learn what has changed in the client's life and keep the process preference honored.
- Present (first meeting) and then execute (every meeting after) the wealth management plan, one recommendation at a time, with the client choosing drop, defer, or do.

## Trigger

- **First RPM:** ninety days after CCM4 (SOP 16), once the expert-network meeting (SOP 32) has produced the draft advanced-planning plan.
- **Subsequent RPMs:** at the cadence the client stated in Discovery (quarterly, semi-annual, or annual) and confirmed at CCM4; never less than annually.
- An RPM is also convened early when a life event in SOP 22 changes the plan.

## Roles

Owner: LA · Does: AA (progress report, tracker extract, previous-quarter figures, packet), CSA (agenda, confirmation, logistics, [CRM], follow-through on asks), SA (co-attends; leads routine RPMs once the relationship-manager test in SOP 73 is met) · Consulted: ISB / EEN expert for any complex recommendation; [Firm Compliance] for the fee and disclosure items.

## Inputs

- Total Client Profile (SOP 11) and the last recap.
- Investment plan and IPS (SOP 13); previous-quarter closing statements or the reporting package (SOP 24).
- The advanced-planning plan (SOP 30) — for the first RPM, the draft from the expert-network meeting; afterwards the current tracker (T10).
- Implementation tracker (T07) for anything still in transfer.
- T05 (who may attend, what may be shared, whether asks are permitted) and the personality note (SOP 04).
- Annual-calendar items due this quarter (T15).

## Prep checklist

- [ ] SOP 05 prep complete (agenda, letter, packet, attendees, roles, no surprises).
- [ ] Progress report drafted against the goals in the profile, using the previous quarter's closing figures.
- [ ] Tracker current: every item has a status and a next step; completed items since last meeting listed.
- [ ] Any plan or recommendation to be presented has been peer-reviewed and signed off (see Procedure step 4).
- [ ] Expert available — in person or by phone — for any complex recommendation on the agenda.
- [ ] Annual items due: fee conversation packet; IPS review; T05 re-scope; survey reminder.
- [ ] Second-chair or rotation designation recorded if this is the year's development slot.
- [ ] Asks permitted per T05; the ideal-client description ready for the introductions ask.

## Procedure

1. **Open per SOP 05.** Greet by name, reinforce the client's personality note, walk the agenda, ask for additions. Hold questions the progress report will answer in sequence.
2. **Ask what has changed.**

    !!! script "Say something like"
        "What has happened since we last got together?" — then, if needed: "Has anything happened in your life or the family's that affects your finances and that I should know about?"

    Listen for career and business changes, births, marriages, divorces, deaths, health, disputes, sales. Note anything that requires a change to the investment plan or the advanced-planning plan.
3. **Report progress.** Walk the progress report: goals, the investment plan, the previous quarter's results placed in the long-term frame. Reinforce the long view whenever short-term performance is down.
4. **Present the advanced-planning plan (first RPM).** Confirm the plan has been peer-reviewed and signed off before it enters the room.

    !!! extension "Beyond Bowen"
        Every plan or proposal is reviewed by a second professional (SA, AA, or a bench expert) and signed off by the LA before it is presented. No exceptions for time pressure.

    Then, in order: the four areas at a high level; the highest-priority items in each; the specific challenges the client faces and how the recommended solutions address them; then each recommendation with as much detail as the client needs to decide.
5. **Coach each recommendation to a decision.** For each item the client chooses **drop**, **defer**, or **do** (Mode B adds **refer to family team**). Take complete notes of the response and the reasoning. Summarize the next step for every item before moving on.
6. **Review the tracker (subsequent RPMs).** Completed items acknowledged; in-progress items with owner and date; future items re-prioritized. Keep the high-priority items prominent and educate the client on the aspects of the plan that matter now. For a complex item — a family partnership, a dynasty trust, a business-succession structure via [ESOP Co.] — the relevant expert explains it directly.
7. **Run the consequences check before anything long-term is executed.**

    !!! extension "Beyond Bowen"
        Before a perpetual or multi-generational structure moves from "do" to execution, ask together: what unintended consequences could this have for the people it is meant to benefit; have we taken the long view and hastened slowly; what harm might we do before we try to do good; how will the beneficiaries experience this; what happens if it turns out to be wrong? Record the answers with the decision.

8. **Pace the conversation to what the client is ready for.**

    !!! extension "Beyond Bowen"
        Alternate questions that supply knowledge with questions that call for courage — the ones the client already knows the answer to but has not acted on. When the family is not ready, most of the time the right move is to wait; note the item and return to it next meeting.

9. **Answer remaining questions** about the plan.
10. **Make the asks** (SOP 50): first additional assets, then introductions. Thank the client whatever the answer.
11. **Annual items when due.** Hold the fee conversation — in writing, in context, with the client leading the questions; review the IPS; re-scope T05; confirm the survey is on its way.

    !!! extension "Beyond Bowen"
        Discuss fees at least once a year, in a written form the client can keep, put in the context of what the practice does for the family, and treat the client's questions as a normal part of the relationship rather than a wedge.

12. **Close per SOP 05.** Summarize decisions and next steps by owner and date; ask what would make the next meeting better; schedule it in the room.

## Follow-up

- Within 24 hours: written recap and confirmation (T01); [CRM] log of life changes, decisions, asks and outcomes; handwritten thank-you for any introduction.
- Within 48 hours: tracker (T10) updated; expert follow-ups assigned; implementation items opened on T07.
- Within one week: calls to any introduced prospect; report back to the client on the outcome; any "refer to family team" item packaged for the FCT.
- Before the next RPM: every "do" item has visible progress or a documented reason it does not.

## Do / Don't

**Do**

- Use the most recent quarter-end figures; never present stale numbers.
- Tell the client of any major change before the meeting.
- Bring the expert into the room for anything complex rather than paraphrasing them.
- Treat every RPM as the first: same preparation, same energy.
- Let the client decide; coaching is not persuading.

**Don't**

- Don't skip the asks because the meeting ran long — move them earlier next time.
- Don't execute a long-term structure in the same meeting it was first agreed; the consequences check and counsel come first.
- Don't let the tracker become a list of everything; it stays prioritized by impact on the client's life.
- Don't present a plan nobody else has reviewed.

## Mode A / Mode B

### Mode A — whole family

- The RPM for a household sits alongside the family-level annual meeting (SOP 43); items that belong to the family as a whole move to the family-level tracker.
- Trust and entity recommendations are routed to the trustee or decision-maker named on T14 before being recorded as "do."
- The introductions ask is framed as other members or branches the family would like the practice to serve, via the elders or council where they exist.

### Mode B — individual within a larger team

- The agenda adds a coordination update: changes on the family side that affect this individual.
- Recommendations touching family-level structures are drafted as items to take to family counsel or the FCT — the fourth coaching outcome, **refer to family team** — and are not executed by the Advisor.
- Asks are limited to assets within the individual's own discretion and to introductions made through the individual, per SOP 02 and SOP 50; nothing is solicited around the FCT.
- Recap distribution follows the consent on T05.

## Metrics

- Share of RPMs held within the client's stated cadence (target 100%).
- Share of tracker items with a recorded client decision and a dated next step (target 100%).
- Asks made per RPM (target: both, whenever T05 permits).
